# Delivery checks

Compare the final artwork with the approved proposal and source ledger. Inspect the AI master in Illustrator and view the actual exports separately. Use this loop after a defect is found: record it, fix the master, regenerate affected formats, reopen those files and check the specific fix and its surroundings.

## Three reviews

| Review | Perform these checks | Failure examples worth catching |
|---|---|---|
| Scientific | Trace each depicted method, branch, estimate and conclusion to a ledger entry. Check population, design, measurement, comparator, denominator, units, direction, uncertainty and who supplied the analysis. Confirm which arrows show procedure, comparison, temporal sequence, evidence or regulation. Check the omission table against material negative, conflicting or sensitivity findings. | A patient questionnaire is drawn as a laboratory diagnosis. An algorithm evaluated in one cohort branches from both. A ratio is plotted on a log-ratio axis. Importance ranks imply unsupported differences in magnitude. |
| Visual | View each export at full canvas and intended publication/display size. Check the focal point, reading order, hierarchy, contrast, font size, glyphs including subscripts, white space, line endpoints, clipping and collisions. Check how color-coded groups read in grayscale or with color-vision differences. Use text labels, shapes or line styles where color carries meaning. | Decorative avatars leave the score derivation unexplained. A confidence interval overlaps another label. A line implies causation. A missing subscript appears only in the exported PDF. |
| Editability and output | Reopen the saved AI in Illustrator. Select a representative path, text frame and major group in their named layers. Confirm bounds, live text, fonts, artboard and expected vector geometry. Open PNG and PDF and any requested additional format. Compare labels, values, positions and finish to the master. Confirm page count, physical size or pixel dimensions, resolution and vector/raster content as appropriate. | A placed preview masquerades as editable paths. One export contains an earlier draft. A symbol shifts on PDF export. A large PNG looks fine on a monitor but scales to unreadable labels at the intended size. |

**Independent reading check.** Give a reader or a fresh reviewer only the rendered figure and its intended size. Ask who or what was studied, how the comparison or synthesis was formed, what was found, and how far the conclusion can extend. Note exactly where they had to guess. Put back the missing bridge or reorganize the scene rather than adding a paragraph. If no independent reader is available, report a pending independent check and distinguish it from your own inspection.

**Journal-specific check.** Reopen the target's current instructions and compare them to the delivered formats, dimensions, legibility and AI-assistance policy. Record any unverified or unresolved requirement. A vector export alone cannot establish that a journal will accept the figure.

## Handoff record

Give the author the AI, PNG preview, vector PDF and the journal-required export if different. Confirm all previews match the master revision. Include a short report with actual results.

| Field | Enter the observed result |
|---|---|
| Source and decision | Manuscript version, proposal version, approved takeaway and date or message of author approval |
| Production route | AI application and server versions, OS, MCP operations used, any consented native-script steps and the basis for that consent |
| Files delivered | Name, purpose and version of each file, final dimensions and relevant format settings |
| Scientific check | Source locations for key claims, scale and uncertainty checks, evidence boundary or outstanding scientific question |
| Visual check | Which rendered files and sizes were viewed, independent reader's response, issues fixed and rechecked |
| Editability check | Which reopened AI layers, paths, groups and text were examined, any limitations in derivative formats |
| Submission and author follow-up | Target journal and instructions checked, any unresolved conditions or requested author decision. If journal-neutral, state the actual intended use and display size. |
